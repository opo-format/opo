# Governance

- **Maintainer:** one maintainer leads the OPO format, decides, and merges (decision 0012).
- **Decisions** are recorded in `decisions/` (decision 0001). Anyone, human or agent, may propose a decision record by pull request; it takes effect when the maintainer accepts it.
- **Changes to the standard** follow the proposal stages in `docs/workflow.md` (decision 0018).
- **AI agents** follow `AGENTS.md`. They may push and merge their own work (decision 0019); normative changes always need the maintainer's explicit approval first.
- **Licensing:** see `LICENSE`. Contributors agree to license their contributions under the licence of the files they change; a contributor licence agreement will be in place before outside contributions are accepted.
- **Growing:** when a second regular contributor joins, this document gains code owners per area and two-person review for normative changes; when vendors join, a steering group and an extension naming policy (vendor prefixes) are added.
