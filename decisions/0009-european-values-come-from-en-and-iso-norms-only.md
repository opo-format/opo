# 0009. European values come from EN and ISO norms only

## Context

OPO is international; national norms (DIN, NF, UNE…) fragment the library.

## Decision

European values come only from EN and ISO norms. Where none exists, sizes are generic and labelled 'not a norm' and rule sets stay empty; projects add national rules as their own layers. EU accessibility uses ISO 21542 until prEN 17210 is published. UK and US are separate markets.

## Consequences

Some EU rule sets are empty by design.
