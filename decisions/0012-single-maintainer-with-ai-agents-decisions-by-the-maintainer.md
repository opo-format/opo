# 0012. Single maintainer with AI agents; decisions by the maintainer

## Context

For the next year the project is run by one person working with AI agents.

## Decision

The maintainer decides and records decisions (0001). AI agents do most of the work through issues and pull requests; CI is the gate; the maintainer merges, and alone approves anything normative (spec, schema, decisions). The process stays light but is written so a core team or community can join later without rework.

## Consequences

Revisit when a second regular contributor joins (code owners, two-person review for normative changes).
